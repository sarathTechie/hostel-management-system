# Student quick guide

## Sign in

Use your hostel username or roll number and the password provided by the hostel office. Five failed attempts temporarily lock the account. Contact the office if access needs to be restored.

## Your room, fees and notices

- **My room** shows your active block, floor, room, bed and allocation date.
- **Fee management** shows only your own fee dues and payment history. Use **Print** beside a receipt to open a printable receipt page.
- **Notice board** shows notices that are published and not expired.
- **Mess menu** shows published menu weeks. Choose a meal's feedback action to submit a 1–5 rating and optional comment.

## Complaints and leave

- In **Complaints**, choose a category, describe the problem and include its location. Follow the status and history from the same workspace.
- In **Leave requests**, include a reason and an end time later than the start time. Your request remains Pending until a warden approves or rejects it.

Your profile and requests are restricted to your account. Select your profile at the bottom of the sidebar to sign out.
